Accessibility Mac contrast sampling — 2026-08-25

Method

- Resolve AppKit system colors separately under Aqua and Dark Aqua without
  changing the host appearance.
- Sample uniform 2-by-2 background regions from the final Computer Use
  screenshots. The capture service supplied JPEGs; the checked-in PNGs are
  lossless conversions of those rendered captures, so minor JPEG variation is
  expected.
- Calculate WCAG relative luminance against the rendered black foreground.

Pre-fix reproduced samples

- Onboarding accent RGB 0,128,254 with white text: 3.81:1.
- Prominent-control blue RGB 3,120,254 with white text: 4.10:1.
- A 50%-white stage direction over the former 40%-yellow highlight on black:
  approximately 2.78:1.

Final semantic system colors

- NSColor.systemCyan under Aqua: RGB 0,192,232; black contrast 9.71:1.
- NSColor.systemCyan under Dark Aqua: RGB 60,211,254; black contrast 11.94:1.
- NSColor.labelColor / windowBackgroundColor under Aqua: black / white.
- NSColor.labelColor / windowBackgroundColor under Dark Aqua: white /
  RGB 30,30,30.

Source math for the default translucent prompt

- White at 50% over black is approximately 5.32:1.
- Black at 85% over a white desktop leaves RGB 38,38,38; 50%-white content
  over that background is approximately 4.89:1.
- Increase Contrast or Reduce Transparency now forces the prompt, control bar,
  and time badge backgrounds to opaque black and disables focus-edge dimming.
