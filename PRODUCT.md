# Product

> Live product contract. The repository and Issue #2 own implementation and delivery state; update this file when Kyuva's audience, purpose, or product principles change.

## Register

product

## Users

Kyuva serves solo creators, presenters, educators, speakers, and professionals who need to deliver prepared words naturally. On Mac they work beside a camera during calls, webinars, and presentations. On iPhone they may hold the device or mount it near a lens. They want to move from text to confident delivery in seconds, without learning a production suite or trusting their script to an account-backed service.

## Product Purpose

Kyuva is the shortest private path from a script to a calm, readable prompt across Apple devices. Success means a first-time user can find or create a script, adjust only what matters, and start presenting without hunting through settings. The prompt must keep attention near the words and lens while local storage and on-device speech remain honest, understandable defaults.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, assured, focused. Kyuva should feel considerate under pressure: precise enough for serious delivery, warm enough for everyday use, and restrained enough to disappear once speaking begins.

## Anti-references

- A Settings-window utility where writing a script feels like configuring software.
- A control-dense production dashboard or generic SaaS card grid.
- Teleprompter.com feature sprawl, account pressure, and paywall-first interruptions.
- Neon cyan gaming controls, decorative gradients, or glass on every surface.
- Hidden core actions that require hover, menus, or prior product knowledge.

## Design Principles

1. **Script first.** The user's words, not the app's configuration, own the largest and clearest surface.
2. **One obvious next action.** Library, editor, and prompt each expose one primary move without hiding essential alternatives.
3. **Calm near the lens.** Prompt chrome recedes; reading position, pace, and state remain legible at a glance.
4. **Progressive control.** Everyday controls stay close. Advanced presentation options remain available without becoming the workflow.
5. **Local-first without ceremony.** Privacy is a dependable product behavior, not a repeated marketing banner.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Kyuva targets WCAG 2.2 AA contrast where platform rendering allows it, supports VoiceOver, Dynamic Type on iPhone, macOS increased contrast, reduced motion, and reduced transparency, and never relies on color alone for state. Phone controls must provide at least 44-point targets. Reading controls must remain usable with long titles, large text, multiple writing systems, and right-to-left script content.
